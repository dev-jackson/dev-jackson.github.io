Frequently asked questions

My reminders don't arrive.

Go to iOS Settings → Notifications → Posol and check they're allowed. Inside the app, Settings → Reminders tells you whether they'll go off and when the next one is.

It misread my prescription.

It can happen: reading is automatic. That's why you confirm what it read before saving, and anything that wasn't read clearly has to be confirmed one by one. Everything can be corrected later from Prescriptions → the medication → Adjust.

I take a pill "as needed".

Turn on "Only as needed" when you create or edit the medication. It has no alarm: it shows up in Today, separately, so you can log it when you take it.

"After dinner" lands at an odd time.

Posol uses your times. Change them in Settings → Your day → When you eat.

The app is in Spanish (or English) and I want the other language.

Posol follows your phone's language. To change only Posol: iOS Settings → Posol → Language.

Is anything stored on the internet?

Your prescriptions, doses and times aren't: they're only on your phone. For the same reason, if you delete the app they're lost: there's no cloud backup yet. The only thing sent is anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off in Settings → Your data.

I want to share my log with my doctor.

Exporting isn't possible yet. If you need it, write to us: it helps us decide what to build first.

Posol helps you remember; it doesn't replace your doctor or pharmacist.
