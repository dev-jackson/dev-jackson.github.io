Posol

Your prescription, on time.

Never wonder whether you took it again, or whether your mom took hers. Take a photo of the prescription and in a minute Posol builds the schedule and reminds you of every dose, until the last day of the treatment.

Download free on the App Store

For iPhone. Free, no ads, no purchases and no account.

Our promises. Nothing is saved until you confirm it. If anything on your prescription isn't read clearly, we point it out so you can check it. Your prescriptions, doses and times stay on your iPhone.

What's included

  • Reads your prescriptionTake a photo of the paper and it recognizes medications, doses, how often and for how many days. You check it before anything is saved.
  • Speaks in meals"After dinner" lands whenever you have dinner, not at a made-up time.
  • Reminds you, even when closedOne reminder per dose. Mark it from the reminder itself, the widget or the Lock Screen.
  • Until the last dayWith two days left it reminds you: that's when people stop most often.
  • Before you run outTell it how many you have at home and the reminder tells you when to buy more.
  • Paper schedulePrint each prescription's schedule with a box per dose, for the fridge or for whoever helps you.

No app? That works too

If the person taking the medication doesn't use a phone, get the printable schedule sheet. It's free and needs nothing installed.
