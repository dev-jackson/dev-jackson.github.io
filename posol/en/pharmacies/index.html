For pharmacies

Many people leave the pharmacy with a prescription for several days and stop halfway once they feel better. Posol is a free app that reads the prescription from a photo and reminds them of every dose, until the last day.

How to help your patients. Print these cards, cut them out and hand them over with the prescription. There are no commissions or agreements: it's a service for your patients. Posol gives no medical advice; that's your job.

Business-card size (85 × 55 mm). You can also point anyone who doesn't use a phone to the printable schedule.
